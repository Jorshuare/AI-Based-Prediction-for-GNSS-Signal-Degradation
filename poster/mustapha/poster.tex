\posec{9-State EKF I Implemented}
\begin{poblock}
State vector:
\[
  \mathbf{x} = [x, y, v_x, v_y, \psi, b, b_{a_x}, b_{a_y}, z]^\top \in \mathbb{R}^9
\]

Adaptive R formula:
\[
  R = R_\mathrm{base} \times (1 + 10 \cdot P_\mathrm{DEGRADED})
\]

When $P=0$: standard EKF ($R = R_\mathrm{base}$)\\
When $P=1$: GPS weight $\approx$ 8\,\% of standard

\medskip
\textbf{Simulated validation:}\\
Fed 60-second blockage sequence; GNSS weight plot showed
expected drop when $P_\mathrm{DEGRADED}$ rose to 1.0 ✓
\end{poblock}

\posec{Dashboard Panel 5 — Fusion Status}
\begin{poblock}
Stacked horizontal bar showing real-time GNSS/IMU weighting:

  \text{IMU\%} = 100 \cdot P_\mathrm{DEGRADED}

Makes the prediction tangible: bar shifts visually from green-GNSS to
blue-IMU as the vehicle enters the building shadow.
\end{poblock}
