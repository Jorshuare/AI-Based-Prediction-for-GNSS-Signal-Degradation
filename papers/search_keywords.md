## HOW TO DOWNLOAD

### Google Scholar
1. Go to scholar.google.com
2. Paste the search string in quotes for exact phrases, e.g.:  
   `"GNSS NLOS multipath detection urban canyon"` + filter: 2015–2026
3. Download PDF → import to NotebookLM

### IEEE Xplore (best for T-ITS / T-VT papers)
1. Go to ieeexplore.ieee.org → Advanced Search
2. Search in Abstract+Title; add filter: **IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems** OR **IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology** OR **IEEE Sensors Journal**

### Scopus / Web of Science
1. Good for GPS Solutions, Journal of Geodesy, Navigation papers
2. Filter: Source title = "GPS Solutions" OR "Journal of Geodesy" OR "Navigation"

### ArXiv
1. arxiv.org/search → cs.RO OR eess.SP
2. Good for recent Transformer + navigation papers and datasets
